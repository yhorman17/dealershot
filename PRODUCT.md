# DealerShot product context

DealerShot is an authenticated automotive operations product for dealership staff, inventory managers, photographers, sales managers, dealer administrators, and platform owners. It keeps vehicle intake, guided photography, image processing, supporting documents, and exports in one tenant-protected workspace.

The product must optimize for speed, clarity, confidence, and low-friction repetition during a busy dealership workday. It is used on desktop inside a dealership and on mobile outdoors beside vehicles, so controls must remain legible in glare, touch targets must be comfortable, and the interface must preserve useful density without becoming cluttered.

Brand personality: premium, modern, automotive, operational, trustworthy, clean, fast, and deliberate.

The product must not feel like a generic SaaS dashboard or an AI-generated Tailwind template. Avoid excessive gradients, neon effects, pervasive glassmorphism, random bright colors, toy-like rounded cards, huge empty spaces, heavy shadows, marketing typography inside the app, thin low-contrast text, repetitive card grids, or ornamental animation.

Security and functional behavior are product requirements, not implementation details. Active tenant authorization, protected roles, Supabase RLS and Storage rules, auth state preservation, routes, data models, health checks, Nitro runtime behavior, and DigitalOcean compatibility must remain intact through visual work.
